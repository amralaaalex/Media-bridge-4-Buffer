Privacy

media-bridge keeps as little as it can, for as short a time as it can.

What we store

  • Your image, at a public but unguessable address. It has to be public so Buffer can fetch it when your post goes out.
  • Your Buffer account and organization IDs, to know which files are yours.
  • Buffer access tokens, encrypted, so we can create your post and check whether it was sent.
  • The Buffer post ID, channel ID, post status and timestamps for each image, plus a log of when and why each image was deleted.

We don't store your post text, your password, or anything from your social accounts.

How long we keep your image

  • Until Buffer sends your post. Then we delete it automatically.
  • If you delete the post in Buffer, we delete the image too.
  • Images you upload but never post are deleted after {{ unattached_ttl_hours }} hours.
  • Drafts and posts that never publish are deleted after at most {{ media_ttl_days }} days.

Deleting things yourself

  • Delete now on any image removes it immediately.
  • Delete my data (in the account menu) removes every image and every record about you. Your posts in Buffer are not touched.
  • To revoke our access to Buffer, remove media-bridge from the connected apps in your Buffer account settings.

Who we share with

Images are stored with Cloudflare R2 and records with Neon Postgres (Frankfurt). Buffer fetches your image to publish it. Nobody else gets your data, and we don't use analytics or ads.

media-bridge is an independent project built on the Buffer API for the Buffer Commit contest. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Buffer. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
