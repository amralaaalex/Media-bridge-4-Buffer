For people who post with Buffer

Post images to Buffer without hosting them yourself.

Buffer's API only accepts images that are already online somewhere, and a scheduled post breaks if that file disappears before it publishes. media-bridge hosts the image for you, keeps it exactly until Buffer sends your post, then deletes it.
